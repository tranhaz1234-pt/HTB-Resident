== Description ==

The Coworking Office theme is expertly crafted for shared workspaces, startup hubs, and flexible office environments that require a polished and organized online presence. It features dedicated sections for workspace listings, membership plans, team profiles, and booking details, making it ideal for agencies, coworking cafes, and hot desking solutions. The layout promotes easy navigation and a tidy content display, suitable for coworking space websites, business centers, and rental office platforms. With a lightweight structure that ensures efficient performance and user-friendly customization options, this theme supports responsive design, modern layouts, typography control, and integration with essential plugins. It is perfect for office rental websites, meeting room bookings, shared workspace directories, and startup communities. Its compatibility with the block editor, SEO-friendly framework, fast loading speeds, and minimalist design elements allow the Coworking Office theme to effectively showcase workspace services without unnecessary complexity.

= Installation =
	
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in coworking-office in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on 'Install' button to install and 'Activate' button to use.
